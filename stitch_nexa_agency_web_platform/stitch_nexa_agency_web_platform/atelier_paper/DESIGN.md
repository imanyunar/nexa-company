## Brand & Style

This design system serves as the tactile, paper-grade counterpart to architectural slate interfaces. Crafted for high-end design consultancies, brand ateliers, and multidisciplinary technology practices, it evokes the quiet authority of heavy archival stock, precision Swiss grid typography, and bespoke craftsmanship.

### Personality & Demeanor
- **Tactile & Archival:** The aesthetic rejects glossy plastics, excessive glass blurs, and neon gradients in favor of warm, uncoated paper hues and physical ink depth.
- **Authoritative & Quiet:** Confidence conveyed through generous whitespace, razor-sharp baseline grids, and disciplined typographic scale rather than decorative gimmickry.
- **Architectural Craft:** Every line, margin, and boundary reflects deliberate human intent, structural clarity, and editorial prestige.

### Aesthetic Foundation
A hybrid of **Swiss International Typographic Style** and **Tactile Editorial Minimalism**. Surfaces feel like 120gsm unbleached cotton paper, typography reads like custom-set lead type, and borders act as precise hairline rules rather than heavy digital enclosures.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in unbleached paper stocks, mineral slates, and rich carbon ink. Contrast is tuned for sustained legibility and an authentic editorial tone.

### Palette Architecture
- **Canvas & Surfaces:**
  - Base Surface (`#FBFBFA`): Warm, unbleached heavy paper. Primary application background.
  - Surface Subdued (`#F4F4F0`): Slightly deeper parchment used for cards, secondary toolbars, and grouped containers.
  - Surface Recessed (`#EAEAE5`): Grounding neutral for active states, nested card wells, and structural table headers.
- **Ink & Slates (Typography & Accents):**
  - Primary Carbon (`#111417`): Deep ink-black with an imperceptible warm undertone. Used for display titles, primary interactive fills, and primary labels.
  - Secondary Slate (`#2D3139`): Grounded charcoal for running text, subheads, and active icons.
  - Muted Charcoal (`#646A74`): Balanced neutral for secondary metadata, timestamps, captions, and structural guidelines.
- **Borders & Rules:**
  - Hairline Rule (`#E2E2DC`): Exact, subtle border stock defining frames without visual friction.
  - Hairline Strong (`#D1D1C8`): Elevated dividers and active element contours.
- **Editorial Accent:**
  - Archival Umber (`#8C6A43`): A muted, warm bronze/raw umber accent strictly reserved for refined emphasis, badge indicators, and verified hallmarks.

## Typography

The typographic treatment relies uniformly on **Plus Jakarta Sans**, tuned with disciplined tracking and strict line-height baselines. The letterforms combine geometric cleanliness with subtle humanist warmth, mirroring architectural typesetting.

### Typographic Principles
- **Negative Tracking on Headlines:** Tightened tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.035em`) transforms larger scales into authoritative typographic lockups.
- **Editorial Micro-Labels:** Small UI labels (`label-md` and `label-sm`) employ uppercase styling paired with expanded letter spacing (`+0.04em` to `+0.08em`), evoking technical colophons and ledger annotations.
- **Pacing & Text Scansion:** Body copy is calibrated at `15px / 24px` to create effortless readability against off-white grounds.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts conform to an asymmetric 12-column structural grid system built on an 8px baseline rhythm (with 4px half-steps for micro-components).

### Grid & Form Factors
- **Desktop (>= 1280px):** 12-column grid, max-width `1440px`, centered. `gutter-lg` (40px), `margin-lg` (64px). Ideal for expansive agency case studies, dual-pane studio indices, and wide data spreads.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8-column grid. `gutter` (24px), `margin` (32px). Secondary panels stack or collapse into off-canvas drawers.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid grid. `gutter-sm` (16px), `margin-sm` (16px). Grids collapse into vertical narrative flows with strict full-bleed or inset cards.

### Spacing Philosophy
Negative space is treated as an active material component. Elements are separated using deliberate breathing room (`space-xl`, `space-2xl`) rather than stacked divider lines, ensuring a serene, curated cadence throughout the interface.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system deliberately shuns synthetic drop shadows, floating layers, and diffuse glows. Elevation is achieved through **tonal stacking** and **hairline boundaries**.

### Depth Hierarchy
1. **Canvas (Ground 0):** `#FBFBFA` — The underlying sheet.
2. **Elevated Inset / Surface (Ground 1):** `#F4F4F0` bounded by a 1px solid `#E2E2DC` hairline border. Used for interactive modules, work showcases, and grouped meta-data.
3. **Interactive & Recessed (Ground 2):** `#EAEAE5` with `#D1D1C8` boundary stroke. Used for active input troughs, pressed states, and nested media frames.
4. **Overlays & Modals:** Solid `#FBFBFA` surface encased in a crisp double-edge rule (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 1px #E2E2DC, 0 8px 24px -4px rgba(17, 20, 23, 0.06)`). Shadows are strictly mineral-tinted, sharp, and heavily restrained.

## Shapes

The geometric framework utilizes an understated, minimal 4px radius (`roundedness: 1`), embodying precision paper trim and architectural drafting edges.

### Radius Execution
- **Standard UI Elements (`4px / 0.25rem`):** Buttons, inputs, chips, checkboxes, table rows, and popovers.
- **Card & Content Containers (`8px / 0.5rem`):** Medium cards and modular content frames use `rounded-lg` for subtle softness while retaining mechanical crispness.
- **Special Elements (`0px`):** Full-bleed image viewports, structural split-screen borders, and rule dividers maintain razor-sharp 90-degree junctions.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#111417`, foreground `#FBFBFA`, `4px` radius. Hover state transitions to `#2D3139`. Height: `40px` (standard), `48px` (prominent). Padding: `0 20px`. Typography: `title-md`.
- **Secondary / Outline:** Background `transparent`, border `1px solid #E2E2DC`, text `#111417`. Hover: background `#F4F4F0`, border `#D1D1C8`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** Text `#2D3139`, no border, padding `0 12px`. Hover: background `#F4F4F0`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status / Meta Chips:** Background `#F4F4F0`, border `1px solid #E2E2DC`, text `#2D3139`. Radius: `4px`. Typography: `label-sm` (uppercase).
- **Accent Badge:** Background `rgba(140, 106, 67, 0.08)`, border `1px solid rgba(140, 106, 67, 0.25)`, text `#8C6A43`.

### Input Fields
- **Default State:** Background `#FBFBFA`, border `1px solid #E2E2DC`, text `#111417`, placeholder `#646A74`. Radius `4px`. Height `44px`, padding `0 14px`.
- **Focus State:** Border `1px solid #111417`, subtle inner box-shadow `inset 0 0 0 1px #111417`. No colored glows.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Unchecked:** `18px × 18px` box, background `#FBFBFA`, border `1.5px solid #D1D1C8`, radius `3px` (checkbox) or circular (radio).
- **Checked:** Background `#111417`, border `1.5px solid #111417`. Check icon in `#FBFBFA`.

### Cards & Case Modules
- Surface `#F4F4F0`, border `1px solid #E2E2DC`, radius `8px`, inner padding `24px`.
- Interactive hover: border transitions cleanly to `#2D3139` with no vertical bounce or artificial lift.

### Lists & Tables
- Divided with `1px solid #E2E2DC` horizontal rules.
- Column headers set in `label-sm` uppercase `#646A74`.
- Row hover transitions to `#F4F4F0` seamlessly across the full viewport or container width.
