## Brand & Style

The design system projects quiet authority, understated confidence, and artisanal digital craftsmanship. Designed for a high-tier creative agency, the aesthetic bridges Scandinavian minimalism with warm architectural restraint. It avoids tech jargon, heavy neon tropes, and visual noise in favor of intentional whitespace, balanced typography, and considered materiality.

The emotional atmosphere is calm, discerning, and hospitable. The interface frames creative work as a strategic business asset rather than mere decorative artifact. Surfaces feel tangible yet weightless, relying on warm slate fundamentals accented by subdued earthen warmth.

## Colors

The palette is built around deep, warm mineral slates rather than clinical jet blacks, creating a soft nocturnal reading canvas.

- **Primary (`#F4F0EA` — Alabaster Sand):** Used for primary text headings, high-contrast actions, and key brand marks. Softened from pure white to reduce optic fatigue.
- **Secondary (`#D4A373` — Raw Ochre):** A quiet, warm micro-accent. Reserved for status badges, active tab markers, hover states, and key focal anchors.
- **Tertiary (`#8A9A86` — Muted Sage):** Used sparingly for secondary visual accents, validation successes, and organic metadata tags.
- **Neutral (`#14171A` — Deep Slate Ground):** The root canvas tone. Paired with elevated layer stops (`#1C2024` for cards and panels, `#262B30` for hover surfaces, and `#3A4148` for delicate structural dividers).
- **Muted Foreground (`#9E9A93` — Bone Mist):** Dedicated secondary typography tone for body prose, labels, and secondary supporting descriptors.

## Typography

The type system blends the contemporary, sculptural forms of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for prominent headers and focal points with the crisp, neutral utility of **Inter** for sustained reading contexts.

- Headlines rely on negative tracking and generous leading to achieve an editorial, publication-grade tone.
- Text sizing is deliberately spacious: narrative body copy prioritizes line heights of 1.6 to 1.7 to enhance flow across wide grid spans.
- Small labels and category trackers use slightly widened tracking to ensure clean optical separation against dark backgrounds.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is structured around an asymmetrical, wide-canvas 12-column responsive fluid grid designed to display large case imagery, case vignettes, and editorial summaries.

- **Breakpoints:** Mobile (under 768px), Tablet (768px–1024px), Desktop (1025px and up).
- **Margins & Gutters:** Mobile uses a 4-column layout with a 1.25rem outer margin; tablet adapts to 8 columns with 2.5rem margins; desktop deploys 12 columns with 5rem outer padding and a 2.5rem gutter.
- **Rhythm:** Spacing follows an intentional cadence where empty vertical space acts as a framing device. Large portfolio sections leverage `space-xl` (and multiples thereof) to isolate case studies and creative statements.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is built without dramatic drop shadows. The design system uses tonal stepping and delicate, low-contrast ghost borders to communicate layering:

- **Base Ground (`#14171A`):** Canvas backdrop for the core page flow.
- **Level 1 Panels (`#1C2024`):** Cards, case study tiles, and floating sheets. Outlined by a soft 1px perimeter using `rgba(244, 240, 234, 0.08)`.
- **Level 2 Modals & Overlays (`#23282E`):** Navigational drawers and floating menus. Paired with a delicate, ultra-diffused ambient shadow: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.45)`.
- **Interactivity:** Subtle surface brightening on hover (`#262B30`) paired with fine 1px border transitions from 8% to 20% opacity.

## Shapes

The design system adheres to soft architectural tailoring. Elements feature subtle edge treatment (`0.25rem` standard, `0.5rem` for cards and medium surfaces, and `0.75rem` for modals) rather than rounded or pill shapes.

This maintains a grounded, structural feel that echoes physical print portfolios, architectural boards, and high-end editorial layouts. Small chips, tags, and form fields share this restraint to ensure visual consistency across all touchpoints.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid `#F4F0EA` background with `#14171A` text. `0.25rem` border radius, medium weight typography (`label-md`). Hover transitions smoothly to `#E5E0D8`.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Transparent background enclosed by a 1px border of `rgba(244, 240, 234, 0.15)` with `#F4F0EA` text. On hover, fills subtly with `rgba(244, 240, 234, 0.05)` and shifts border to `rgba(244, 240, 234, 0.3)`.
- **Text Link Button:** Understated text in `#D4A373` with a fine baseline underline offset by 4px; on hover, shifts to `#F4F0EA`.

### Cards & Project Showcases
- Surface filled with `#1C2024` and framed with a 1px border (`rgba(244, 240, 234, 0.08)`). Corner radius is strictly `0.5rem`.
- Padding relies on `space-lg` internally. Imagery within cards adheres to a `0.25rem` radius or sits flush to edge dividers.

### Chips & Metadata Tags
- Compact height (28px), with subtle padding (`space-xs` vertically, `space-sm` horizontally).
- Neutral background (`#262B30`) with crisp typography in `#9E9A93` (`label-sm`).
- Active or featured tags introduce a delicate `#D4A373` border and matching text accent.

### Input Fields & Inquiries
- Subtle background (`#171A1D`) with a 1px border (`rgba(244, 240, 234, 0.12)`).
- Padding uses `space-sm` vertically and `space-md` horizontally. Placeholder text is rendered in `#9E9A93`.
- Focus state softens the border to `#D4A373` with zero intrusive outer glow rings.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Crisp 18px geometry with `0.125rem` corner rounding for checkboxes and circular geometry for radios.
- Unselected state uses a 1px border in `rgba(244, 240, 234, 0.2)`.
- Selected state fills with `#D4A373` and displays a `#14171A` checkmark or interior dot.

### Lists & Service Indices
- Editorial horizontal row arrangement separated by delicate 1px border dividers (`rgba(244, 240, 234, 0.06)`).
- Rows expand slightly on hover with a micro-indent transition, shifting text from `#9E9A93` to `#F4F0EA`.
